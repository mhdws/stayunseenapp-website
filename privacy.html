The short version

Stay Unseen’s privacy controls and activity statistics run in your browser. The extension does not use analytics, advertising trackers or automatic uploads of your messages, page contents or browsing history.

Pro licensing is the exception to local-only processing. When you activate a licence, the extension sends your licence key and a random installation ID to the Stay Unseen licence service, which checks the purchase with our payment provider and manages device slots. While Pro is active, the extension also renews its signed licence about once a day so that a refunded or expired purchase stops working. Those two requests are the only ones the extension makes to us.

Media downloads and profile checks also contact the relevant social site or its media servers. Those requests provide the tool you use; they do not send your social content to Stay Unseen’s licence service.

This policy covers Stay Unseen 2.0.0 and this website. It explains local storage, optional Pro activation, media requests and your controls separately.

How the extension uses page data

On supported social websites, Stay Unseen inspects outgoing requests in memory to distinguish read receipts, story/status views, presence and typing signals from normal site activity. Requests can share the same address, so this can involve reading their contents, including fields from chat requests.

For Mark Seen, the extension may use the current conversation’s identifiers and the site’s own read action to send the receipt you request. Follow-status tools read the currently viewed profile’s relationship information and may request it from that site using your existing signed-in session. Download tools locate the selected media on the page or in data supplied by the site.

These features process the information in your browser. They do not send messages, social-account credentials, contact lists, profile contents or downloaded media to Stay Unseen’s licence service. The extension does not save the text of your messages.

Focus mode changes which page elements are visible. Screen privacy applies a local blur and interaction lock; it does not record your screen or upload screenshots.

What stays on your device

The extension uses your browser profile’s local extension storage. It does not use browser sync to upload these settings or statistics. Shared settings update supported tabs within the same profile.

Stored locallyPurpose and retention
Feature and utility preferencesRemember per-platform switches, unread markers, the pill’s visibility, Focus mode, screen privacy, queue preferences and the pause deadline. Preferences remain until changed or extension data is removed.
Activity totals and timesShow blocked-signal and download counters, installation time, recent activity and charts. Hourly activity is limited to 24 hours; the daily series keeps up to seven recorded days. Reset counters clears these activity totals and history.
Recent blocks and diagnostic recordsKeep up to 20 recent blocked-signal records and a rolling log of up to 250 diagnostic entries. Records can include supported-site request endpoints, site identifiers, feature, time and decision metadata. They stay local and are not a general browsing history.
Recent tool resultsShow the latest download, Mark Seen and follow-check status, timestamps, error categories, script versions and limited technical details. These records are replaced as tools run.
Download queueRemember media URLs, filenames, platform, creation time, browser download IDs, status and errors. Completed or cancelled items have their media URLs removed; failed items retain URLs for retry until cleared or removed from the retained history.
Installation and Pro activation dataA random installation ID is created locally, including for Free users. After activation, a short-lived signed licence token and an opaque device identifier are stored, so Pro can be checked locally without contacting us on every use. The token expires and is renewed while Pro is active.

Version 2.0.0 does not retain raw request-body or WebSocket-frame captures and clears older saved captures during migration. Message-text fields are removed from diagnostic summaries. The normal store dashboard does not include the technical diagnostics drawer; support builds can expose local diagnostic and copy controls.

Reset counters is not a full data reset. It does not erase your settings, licence, download queue or every diagnostic record. See how to clear data below.

Pro purchase and licence validation

Core Instagram, Facebook and Messenger privacy does not need a purchase or a Stay Unseen account. An optional Pro purchase is processed by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the checkout’s purchase, email, billing and payment information under its own terms.

The extension’s licence service is hosted on Cloudflare at api.stayunseenapp.com. The extension contacts it when you activate or release a licence, and about once a day while Pro is active to renew the signed licence. Renewal sends only the signed licence token and the random installation ID — nothing about your browsing.

ActionInformation sent
Activate or resubmit a keyThe licence key you enter and a random installation ID.
Release this deviceThe signed licence token already stored on this device and the random installation ID. You are not asked for your licence key again, and nothing about the purchase is changed.

The service checks the purchase with our payment provider and keeps its own record of how many installations a licence covers. The random installation ID is a value the extension makes up; it is not derived from your social account, your hardware, or any device fingerprint, and there is no fingerprinting anywhere in the extension.

After activation, the saved token contains a one-way hash of your installation ID, the plan, the features it covers, and an expiry. The extension does not persist your plain-text purchase key, and the server stores only a one-way hash of it. Ordinary Pro access checks verify the token locally and offline; the extension does not contact us every time you use a feature, and if you are offline your existing Pro access keeps working until the token expires.

Licence requests do not include your messages, photos, contacts, viewed profiles, media URLs, download history or general browsing history. Service providers receive ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and request times, and may process it under their own policies.

Media downloads and profile checks

When you request a download, Stay Unseen retrieves media from the supported site or its content-delivery servers. Those servers can receive your IP address and normal request information, including the site context needed to provide the media. Some tools use the social site’s existing logged-in session.

Downloaded files are saved through your browser or a direct in-page download. The optional queue stores the link and filename locally so it can manage pending items, retry failures and organize files into folders. Stay Unseen does not upload these files to its own service.

Follow-status checks use information about the profile you open on Instagram or TikTok. The extension does not build a server-side follower database or upload a list of the profiles you view.

The social platforms and their media providers have their own data practices. Stay Unseen’s blocking controls do not make you anonymous to the websites you use.

This website and external services

This website does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The interactive demonstrations only change the preview on the page; they do not connect to your installed extension or social accounts.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the normal connection information for those requests. Website images, scripts and video files are served with this site. Video playback is requested when you choose to play; poster images load with the page.

Browser-store, checkout, YouTube and creator links take you to external services when selected. The tutorial does not embed a YouTube player. Those services and the website’s hosting provider process normal web requests under their own policies.

What Stay Unseen does not do

  • Use analytics, advertising trackers or automatic crash-report uploads in the extension.
  • Upload message text, page contents, social-account credentials or browsing history to its licence service.
  • Sell personal data or use your social activity for advertising.
  • Require a Stay Unseen account to use the Free privacy controls.

Your controls and data removal

  • Choose features: turn individual switches on or off, pause protection, hide the pill or disable the extension from your browser.
  • Reset counters: clears activity counters, recent blocks, chart history and download totals. Preferences and Pro activation remain.
  • Clear the queue: cancel unwanted pending items, then use Clear finished to remove completed, cancelled and failed entries. Saved files and your browser’s own download history are managed separately.
  • Release Pro: Release this device contacts the licence service and, after success, removes the local licence token and device identifier. It does not cancel or refund the purchase.
  • Remove all extension data: uninstall Stay Unseen or use your browser’s extension-data controls where available. Files already downloaded to your computer remain until you delete them.
  • Share support information: nothing is automatically sent to support. If you copy a report from a support build and send it to us, review it first. Contacting support shares the email address, message and any files you choose to include.

Release your Pro device slot before uninstalling or clearing extension data. Removing local data does not itself release a provider device slot or erase Lemon Squeezy’s purchase records. Contact support if the old installation is no longer available, or for a request about information you have sent us.
