Basics Is Stay Unseen free?

The core Instagram, Facebook and Messenger protections are free. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks WhatsApp Web, Threads and the on-page tools — media downloads, the Instagram follow status badge, Mark Seen, the status pill, timed pause and the activity telemetry — on up to three devices. There is no subscription.

Pro tools Can I save reels, posts and stories with it?

Yes, with Lifetime Pro. A save control appears on the reel, post video, story slide or voice note you are already watching, and the file lands in your browser's usual Downloads at the quality the player is streaming — nothing is re-encoded. In a story it resolves the slide actually on screen rather than the first one in the tray. It saves the single item in front of you: there is no bulk profile export and nothing is crawled.

Pro tools How does the Instagram follow status badge work?

Open a profile and the badge reports the relationship that page already loaded in your own session: following you back, not following you, or your own profile. It reads what your logged-in browser can already see rather than crawling followers. If the lookup is refused, the badge says unknown instead of claiming that someone does not follow you.

Compatibility Does it work in the Instagram or Facebook mobile app?

No. Stay Unseen works on supported websites opened in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. It cannot reach native iOS or Android apps.

Trust Will it get my account blocked or banned?

Stay Unseen does not log in for you, automate anything, or scrape your account. It filters specific outgoing reports inside your own browser — the same category of tool as a content blocker — and it never adds anything to what you send. The download tools save the media your browser is already playing, one item at a time.

Performance Does it slow the sites down?

No. It cancels those requests before they are made, so there is slightly less network work than usual, not more. The panel and its counters are local, so there is nothing extra to fetch.

Basics Do I have to configure anything?

No. Core protection starts with sensible defaults. Open the panel only when you want to change a site or feature, inspect counters, or activate Lifetime Pro.

Privacy What does it store, and does any of it leave my machine?

It keeps your switches, a blocked count per feature, and a short local log with message text removed. Browsing content stays local. Pro activation sends only the licence key and installation identifiers to the licence service. The privacy policy lists every field.

Honesty Does it hide my online or active status?

No. It hides story and reel views, read receipts and typing indicators. Your active indicator is a different thing and is left untouched — see what it cannot do.
