Before you jump in

A few good
questions.

What stays free?

Instagram Story/reel-view protection, Facebook Story protection, Instagram/Facebook/Messenger read-receipt and typing controls, and native unread markers where supported. Pro adds WhatsApp Web and Threads privacy, Instagram/Facebook/TikTok downloads, Instagram/Threads/TikTok follow badges, Mark Seen and utilities. See the platform guide.

What do Seen and Story protections cover?

Stay Unseen suppresses supported outgoing read receipts, Story/Status views and typing signals in your browser. It does not make you anonymous to the platform or guarantee that every signal is blocked after a site changes. Check published compatibility and the dashboard’s local feature health. TikTok tools do not hide profile visits or read receipts.

Does Stay Unseen guarantee account safety?

No extension can guarantee how a platform will enforce its rules. Stay Unseen changes supported page behavior and network requests, and some tools use your existing signed-in session. It does not ask you to submit social passwords to Stay Unseen. Review the platform’s terms before using its tools.

How do media downloads work?

Downloads retrieve available media directly from the supported site or its media servers. Quality depends on what the platform supplies; HD is not guaranteed. Stay Unseen servers do not proxy the files. TikTok uses clean playback media when available, excludes photo slideshows, and has a 128 MB download limit in Chrome/Edge. The optional queue organizes supported remote media by platform and date.

How does Lifetime Pro work?

The current offer is a one-time $9.99 USD Gumroad purchase for up to three active browser installations. Separate browsers or profiles count separately, even on one computer. The extension activates through the Stay Unseen API, checks signed access locally and periodically renews it online. Lifetime purchase access is not an entitlement that works offline forever.

How do I move Pro to another installation?

In the old dashboard’s licence card, select Release this device, then Release. After success, activate your key in the new installation. Release before uninstalling or clearing extension data. If the old installation is inaccessible, contact support. See the activation guide.

What data leaves the browser?

Licensing sends your key during activation and installation/entitlement information for renewal or release to api.stayunseenapp.com. Public compatibility requests send no licensing credentials or local health evidence. Social messages, credentials and downloaded files are not uploaded to Stay Unseen. Local counters and Live Telemetry remain in the browser. Platform requests still go to the social site or its CDN. Read the Privacy Policy.

What is the difference between Focus Mode and Screen Privacy?

Focus Mode hides supported home feeds and reel surfaces on Instagram, Facebook and Threads while leaving messaging available. Screen Privacy blurs and locks supported Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp Web and Threads pages. The Ghost Pill stays usable to turn it off. It does not hide browser notifications, other applications or TikTok.

Does it work in mobile apps?

Stay Unseen is a desktop browser extension for supported websites, with Chrome, Edge and Firefox builds. It does not control native iOS or Android apps. Features differ by platform; see the setup guide before enabling a tool.

What if a platform changes or a feature says Degraded?

Reload the supported tab after extension updates. Hover, focus or click the health badge beside the feature in your dashboard for the local reason. The Status page separately lists manually verified compatibility and dates; neither counters nor local Working prove what another account sees. See the changelog for changes.
