Get started in 3 simple steps

Stay Unseen works immediately upon install with no login or account required. Complete these quick steps to get the smoothest experience:

01 / PIN

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle icon (Extensions) in your browser’s top-right toolbar, find Stay Unseen, and click the pin icon. This gives you instant 1-click access to your privacy switches.

02 / REFRESH

Reload your open social tabs

If you already had Instagram, WhatsApp Web, Facebook, Messenger, Threads, or TikTok open before installing, please reload those tabs once so the privacy filters can attach.

03 / CONFIGURE

Open your dashboard

Click the Stay Unseen toolbar icon anytime to see your live blocked-signal counters, pause protection temporarily, or configure per-feature toggles.

What is protected right now (Free Tier)

Your Free tier privacy protections are automatically active immediately upon install with no license key or account required:

  • Instagram: View Stories silently without adding your name to viewer lists. Read Direct Messages (DMs) without triggering the "Seen" receipt. Hide the live "typing..." indicator in conversations.
  • Facebook & Messenger: Read messages and chat threads secretly with zero read receipts sent. Suppress typing activity beacons across active chat docks.
  • Keep Unread Markers: Retain native unread styling in your inbox where the site allows it so you never lose track of important messages.
  • 100% Client-Side: These core Instagram and Facebook protections run entirely in your local browser memory and never expire.

Stay Unseen Pro: Full Feature Suite

Upgrade to Stay Unseen Pro to extend your stealth shields to WhatsApp Web and Threads, and unlock all advanced visual privacy, profile intelligence, and media tools:

1. Profile Follow-Status Badges (Instagram, Threads & TikTok)

Know instantly whether someone follows you back directly on their profile page header without having to open and search through follower lists:

  • Instagram: Displays "Follows you", "Doesn’t follow you", or "Your profile" directly beneath their profile handle.
  • Threads: Inspects follow relationships and adds an instant status badge to user profiles.
  • TikTok: Shows "Follows you", "Friends" (mutual follow), or "Doesn’t follow you" on creator profiles.

2. Media Downloaders & Queue Organizer

Save content directly at original player quality with clean, non-intrusive on-page download buttons:

  • Instagram & Facebook Downloader: Download reels, videos, stories, and photos at original quality with one click.
  • TikTok Video Downloader: Download TikTok playback videos cleanly without watermarks.
  • Download Queue & Organizer: Queue multiple items to download in the background and automatically organize saved files into clean Stay Unseen / <platform> / <date> folders on your disk.

3. WhatsApp Web & Threads Stealth Mode

Extend zero-telemetry stealth browsing across your other essential messaging platforms:

  • WhatsApp Web: Read incoming messages and group chats without triggering blue checkmarks. View contact Status updates completely unseen. Hide live typing and audio recording status.
  • Threads: Browse feeds, posts, and direct messages without telemetry beacons, presence tracking, or seen markers.

4. In-Page Privacy & Productivity Utilities

Powerful tools designed for professional workflows and screen privacy:

  • Screen Privacy Mode: Instantly blurs and locks active pages with one click or keyboard shortcut. Perfect for protecting confidential client chats when sharing your screen on Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet, or when working in public cafes. (Zero hover-reveal to prevent accidental exposure).
  • Focus Mode: Hides addictive algorithmic home feeds, reels, and video recommendations on Instagram, Facebook, and Threads while keeping your messaging inbox and direct notifications accessible.
  • Selective "Mark as Seen" Button: Adds a clean button into WhatsApp Web, Messenger, and Instagram chat headers to deliberately mark individual conversation threads as read only when you choose.
  • Floating Ghost Pill: A movable in-page widget floating beside your social apps for 1-click status checking, quick feature toggling, and timed protection pauses (15m, 1h, 24h).
